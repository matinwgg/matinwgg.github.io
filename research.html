Research

My research interests focus on building secure, dependable and privacy-aware intelligent and distributed systems.

Applied Cryptography & Privacy

I am interested in practical cryptographic systems and their deployment in security-sensitive applications: authenticated encryption, password/key derivation, secure payment protocols, zero-knowledge proofs, secure multi-party computation and privacy-preserving computation. A recurring goal is to understand the trade-off between formal security properties, performance and usability.

Machine Learning Security & Trustworthy AI

My interests include adversarial robustness, evasion and poisoning attacks, privacy leakage, federated learning, differential privacy and evaluation of defenses. I am particularly interested in experimentally measuring how security and privacy interventions affect model utility, computational cost and reliability.

Blockchain & Distributed Systems Security

I explore secure distributed state, verifiable data, blockchain-based security mechanisms, consensus under faults and decentralized synchronization. I am interested in systems where cryptographic guarantees and distributed protocols can improve the integrity and auditability of sensitive workflows such as financial transactions and credential management.

Secure Payment Systems

My professional FinTech experience motivates research into authentication, authorization, transaction integrity, idempotency, concurrency control, auditability and secure payment protocols. I am interested in designing financial systems that remain correct under retries, failures, concurrent requests and adversarial conditions.

Software Security & Dependability

I am also interested in secure software engineering, API security, security testing, observability, automated testing and CI/CD-native approaches to software quality. This connects my research interests with my practical experience building and reviewing production-oriented software.

Research approach

I prefer implementation-backed research: formulate a precise question or threat model, implement baselines, design controlled experiments, measure security/performance trade-offs, document limitations and make the artifacts reproducible. My current portfolio uses Python for experimentation and machine learning while expanding into Rust, C++ and other systems-oriented languages for performance and protocol implementations.

Selected research-oriented projects
