Research

Security and dependability of intelligent and distributed systems.

Applied Cryptography & Privacy

Applied cryptography, authenticated encryption, password/key derivation, zero-knowledge proofs, secure multi-party computation and privacy-preserving computation, with an emphasis on practical performance and deployment constraints.

Trustworthy & Adversarial Machine Learning

Adversarial robustness, evasion and poisoning attacks, privacy leakage, federated learning, differential privacy and evaluation methods for dependable ML systems.

Distributed Systems Security

Reliable distributed services, secure coordination, verifiable state, blockchain systems and privacy-preserving protocols. I am particularly interested in the tension between strong security guarantees, performance and usability.

Software Security & Dependability

Security-aware software architecture, API security, secure authentication, testing, observability and CI/CD-native quality assurance.

Current research direction

I am developing implementation-backed research artifacts so that hypotheses can be evaluated experimentally, with reproducible code, threat models, baselines and quantitative measurements.
